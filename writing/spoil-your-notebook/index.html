Spoil Your Notebook

Everyone knows the feeling of a brand new notebook. The pages are clean, the spine is stiff, and somehow that makes it harder to write in. You want your handwriting to be neat, your headings straight, and your first page to be perfect. So you wait for the right day, the right mood, the right topic. Often the notebook sits unused.

Here is a simple fix: spoil it on purpose.

On the very first page, before you write a single real note, scribble all over it. Doodle, draw spirals, test your pen, write your name ten times, write nonsense, cross things out. Make it messy. The point is to break the spell. Once page one is already ruined, the rest of the notebook stops feeling precious. You can't ruin something that's already ruined, so you stop worrying about mistakes and start thinking about the content.

I noticed that when I stopped trying to keep my notes perfect, I actually wrote more. I wrote faster and crossed things out without guilt. A notebook that gets used is worth far more than one that stays neat and empty.

So try it with your next notebook. Open it, take your pen, and wreck page one. Then turn the page and just write.

- Dev Chauhan
